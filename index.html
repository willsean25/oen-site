For Employees

Getting your safety glasses

If your employer offers a program through OEN, here's how to get started.

Schedule your appointment

Call or email us and we'll find a time that works around your shift.

Contact us to schedule →

Complete your paperwork

We'll send you your patient forms before your visit, along with a secure way to share your current prescription.

Get fitted

We'll measure and fit your frames, in person or through a guided video session, and order your glasses.

What to bring: your current glasses (if you have them), your employee ID, and your most recent prescription if you haven't already shared it with us.
About

Focused on eye safety at work

Occupational Eyecare Network is a Michigan company dedicated to one thing: making it simple for employers to provide prescription safety eyewear to the people who need it.

Our team brings years of experience running eyewear programs for manufacturing employers, including coordinating panel doctors, fittings, and program billing. We combine that experience with flexible scheduling, simple paperwork, and virtual fittings so employees spend less time away from work.
